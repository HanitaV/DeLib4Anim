# Narrative Archetypes, Hook Formulas & Script Review Model

## 1. Three Video Shapes

Select one shape before writing any scene:

| Shape | Runtime | Typical WPM | Chapters | Opening Job | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shape 1: Concept Mystery Essay** | 5–12 min | 135–142 | 4–7 chapters | Shatter a familiar mental model + pose 2–3 open questions inside 35s | Counter-intuitive topics (quantum, entropy, relativity, Fourier) |
| **Shape 2: Foundational Course Pillar** | 15–30 min | 140–148 | 8–14 chapters | Pair a hands-on tabletop action with a cosmic paradox inside 60s | Systematic foundations (Newtonian mechanics, electromagnetism, calculus) |
| **Shape 3: Short `stem_engine` Demo** | 20–90 s | 130–140 | 1–4 shots | Hook on the central tension or live visual transformation inside 0–5s | Engine showcases, single-concept shorts (`incline.py`, `chemistry_electron.py`) |

## 2. Six Composable Narrative Patterns (3b1b + Median)

1. **Wrong -> Less Wrong -> Right:** Expose a popular textbook simplification (e.g., Bohr marble orbit), prove why it breaks (Heisenberg + radiation collapse), rebuild from waves/fields.
2. **Mystery -> Investigation -> Resolution:** Open with a paradox (why doesn't the Moon fall?), investigate piece by piece, pay off with a clean geometric synthesis (Newton's cannonball).
3. **Two Perspectives -> Unity:** Show View A (inside the turning car / algebraic) and View B (standing on the sidewalk / geometric), then reveal both describe the exact same physics.
4. **Tabletop -> Cosmic Scale Jump:** Start with an experiment on a desk (ball + paper, coffee cup, ant on ruler) and scale the same law to rockets, ISS orbits, or stellar spectra.
5. **Kinematics -> Dynamics Pivot:** First describe *how* motion looks (position, velocity, acceleration curves), then pivot at the midpoint to *why* motion changes (forces, inertia, interactions).
6. **Build-Up -> Payoff:** Assemble simple rules one at a time (e.g., position -> velocity -> area under curve) until a complex curve or phenomenon falls out effortlessly.

## 3. Eight STEM Hook Formulas

Draft **3 hooks** from 3 *different* formulas below before writing the body:

| # | Formula | Mechanism | Vietnamese Starter Pattern |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Mental-Image Trap** | Name the false picture already in the viewer's head and break it | *"Nếu mình hỏi bạn X trông như thế nào, gần như chắc chắn trong đầu bạn sẽ hiện lên..."* |
| 2 | **Do-It-Right-Now Experiment** | Ask the viewer to perform a 5-second physical test with two hands | *"Bạn thử làm giúp mình một việc rất đơn giản như này: cầm X ở tay trái, Y ở tay phải..."* |
| 3 | **Dual-Scale Paradox** | Contrast a mundane object with a cosmic phenomenon sharing one hidden law | *"Quả táo rời cành thì rơi xuống đất, còn Mặt Trăng cũng bị kéo vào nhưng hàng tỷ năm vẫn lơ lửng..."* |
| 4 | **Bodily Sensation Anomaly** | Point to a physical feeling everyone knows that contradicts common sense | *"Khi máy bay lao trên đường băng, lưng bạn bị ép chặt vào ghế. Nhưng ở độ cao 10 cây số với tốc độ 900 km/h, bạn lại không cảm thấy gì..."* |
| 5 | **Everyday Tech Mystery** | Anchor an abstract theorem in light, neon lamps, phones, or GPS | *"Mỗi dải sáng từ đèn neon hay màu sắc trong quang phổ các ngôi sao đều bắt nguồn từ..."* |
| 6 | **Counter-Intuitive Limit** | Push a rule to an extreme (zero friction, vacuum, 1/100 billionth of a second) | *"Nếu kéo suy luận này đến cùng... nó sẽ đâm sầm vào hạt nhân chỉ trong một phần trăm tỷ giây."* |
| 7 | **Historical Impasse** | Frame the problem as a trap that cornered classical science | *"Vật lý cổ điển rơi vào một thế tiến thoái lưỡng nan..."* |
| 8 | **Visual Prediction Bet** | Force the viewer to bet on an outcome before revealing motion (great for 20–60s demos) | *"Nếu thả hai viên bi cùng lúc, một rơi thẳng và một bắn ngang, viên nào chạm đất trước?"* |

### Hook Scoring Rubric (Score `/10`; Keep `>= 7/10`)
- **Misconception / Curiosity Gap (0–3):** Does it make the viewer silently guess an answer or feel a contradiction?
- **Visualizability (0–3):** Can `stem-storyboard-director` stage a clear visual in the first 3 seconds?
- **Zero-Fluff Speed (0–2):** Does it skip greetings ("Xin chào các bạn đã quay trở lại kênh...") and land the tension within 1–2 sentences?
- **Promise Clarity (0–2):** Does it connect directly to what the rest of the video actually proves?

## 4. Mode C: 3-Cause Script Review & Rescue Model

When reviewing an existing script or diagnosing viewer drop-off, audit beat by beat using only these three causes:

| Cause | Symptom in Script | Severity Guide | Concrete Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Logic Break** | Beat ends on A, next beat opens on C; unintroduced technical term; chapter ends without a bridge; open loop forgotten | **HIGH** if viewer loses causal thread; **MEDIUM** if rough jump | Add a 1-sentence causal bridge or reorder along the dependency staircase |
| **2. Density / Redundancy Drop** | VO repeats what is already written on screen; empty deictic (*"như bạn thấy"*); two paragraphs circling one simple claim | **HIGH** in first 60s; **MEDIUM** mid-video | Cut verbal repetition; make screen *show* while VO explains *why* |
| **3. Unspeakable / AI Line** | Sentence >38 words; raw LaTeX in VO; em-dashes; *"không chỉ X mà còn Y"*; passive textbook tone | **MEDIUM–HIGH** whenever rhythm stalls | Split into two short spoken clauses; translate math to plain speech; scrub AI tells |

**Review Rules:**
- Quote the original line beside your proposed fix; never silently delete the author's real data or argument.
- If a tangent carries prerequisite logic needed for the climax, keep it; if it only decorates, mark it `[CUTTABLE]`.
